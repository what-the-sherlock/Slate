# Company history

Kestrel Freight was founded in 2019 in Rotterdam by Ines Okafor and Tomasz Wierzbicki, who met while working at a port logistics company.

In 2022 Kestrel raised an 18 million euro Series A led by Northgate Ventures. The Lisbon office opened in 2023. Today the company has about 120 employees.
