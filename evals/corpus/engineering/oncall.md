# On-call

## Rotation

The on-call rotation is weekly and hands over every Monday at 09:00 CET. Pages arrive through Beacon, our paging tool.

## Response times

A SEV1 page must be acknowledged within 5 minutes and a SEV2 within 30 minutes. If a SEV1 isn't acknowledged within 15 minutes, Beacon escalates to the engineering manager on duty.

## Compensation

Engineers on call receive an allowance of 40 euros per day of the rotation, plus time off in lieu for every page handled between 22:00 and 07:00.
