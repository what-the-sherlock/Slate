# Customer FAQ

## How long do tracking links work?

Public tracking links expire 90 days after delivery.

## Can I export my data?

Yes. Shipments, invoices and events export as CSV or JSON from Settings, Data export.

## Which carriers do you support?

Kestrel integrates with 140 carriers across Europe and North America.

## How do I rotate an API key?

Go to Settings, API keys, and choose Rotate. The old key keeps working for 24 hours so you can deploy the new one.
