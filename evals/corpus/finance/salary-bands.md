# Salary bands 2026

Confidential: People Operations and managers only.

- L3 software engineer: 62,000 to 78,000 euros.
- L4 software engineer: 78,000 to 96,000 euros.
- L5 senior software engineer: 96,000 to 120,000 euros.

Bands are reviewed every October against the Radford benchmark.
