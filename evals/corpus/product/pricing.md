# Pricing

## Plans

- **Starter**: $49 per month, up to 200 shipments.
- **Growth**: $299 per month, up to 5,000 shipments.
- **Enterprise**: custom pricing, unlimited shipments and a dedicated success manager.

## Discounts and overages

Paying annually saves 15%. Shipments above the plan allowance cost $0.08 each. Non-profits get 30% off any plan.
