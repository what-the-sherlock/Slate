# Service level agreement

## Uptime

The Growth plan guarantees 99.9% monthly uptime; Enterprise guarantees 99.95%. Starter has no uptime guarantee.

## Service credits

For every 0.1 percentage point below the guarantee, customers receive a service credit of 10% of that month's fee, up to 50%.

## Support response

Priority-1 tickets get a first response within 1 hour, around the clock, on Enterprise. Growth customers get a first response within 4 business hours.
