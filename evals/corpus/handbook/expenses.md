# Expenses

Submit expenses in Ledgerly, our expense tool, within 30 days of the purchase.

## Approval limits

Your manager can approve expenses up to $2,000 per claim. Anything above $2,000 also needs a director's approval. Software subscriptions always go through the IT team, whatever the amount.

## Travel

The daily per diem for meals while travelling is $65. Book trains for journeys under 5 hours; flights are economy class only.

## Missing receipts

If Ledgerly shows the error ERR_RECEIPT_MISSING, attach a photo of the receipt or a signed declaration. Claims without either are rejected after 14 days.
