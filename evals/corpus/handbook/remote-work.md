# Remote work

Kestrel Freight is remote-first. Our offices in Rotterdam and Lisbon are open to anyone who wants them.

## Equipment and stipends

New joiners receive a one-off home office stipend of $600 for a desk, chair or monitor. A coworking membership is reimbursed up to $150 per month.

## Core hours

Core collaboration hours are 10:00 to 15:00 CET. Outside core hours, schedule your day as you like.

## Working abroad

You may work from another country for up to 30 days per calendar year. Longer stays need approval from People Operations because of tax and payroll rules.
