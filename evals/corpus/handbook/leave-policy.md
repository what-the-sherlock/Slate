# Leave policy

## Paid time off

Every full-time employee at Kestrel Freight gets 25 days of paid time off per calendar year, on top of public holidays in their country of employment. Part-time employees accrue leave pro rata.

## Carryover

Up to 5 unused days carry over into the next year. Carried-over days expire on March 31; anything not taken by then is lost. Ask your manager before planning a single absence longer than 15 working days.

## Sick leave

Sick leave is not capped. For absences longer than 3 consecutive working days, send a doctor's note to People Operations.

## Parental leave

Birthing and non-birthing parents both receive 20 weeks of fully paid parental leave, which can be taken in up to three blocks within 18 months of the birth or adoption.
